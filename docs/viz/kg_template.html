Built from h5_submission.xlsx at method commit __SHA__ (sheet saved __TIME__), __NF__ findings from 13 papers. Every finding sits in exactly one branch, so none is counted twice. Findings of directness class 2 (a lipid changed, the lysosome measured, in the challenge's terms a perturbation of a surrogate A′ with B measured) form the mechanism module between SSP3 and SSP4; every other finding sits under the step its readout measures. MecCog has not published a schema for H5. SSP1, SSP2, the outcome and the ovals marked MecCog are copied from MecCog's mechanism example (drawn after Figure 1 of the Moult and Darden preprint), together with its ratings; SSP3 and SSP4 use the words of the H5 statement; SSP5, its oval and the branch names are ours unless a branch says otherwise. Ratings of the H5 steps follow a rule, not expert judgement: green when at least 75% of the findings support H5 and one of them is class 1 or 2, orange from 50%, red below.
